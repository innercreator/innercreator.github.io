Name That Pro

Guess the athlete from clues

How to play

  1. Pick a sport: soccer, basketball, cricket, tennis or motor racing.
  2. Read the first clue and type who you think the athlete is.
  3. Each wrong guess reveals the next, easier clue. You have five guesses.
  4. Tap Hint to see the next clue early. It uses up one guess.

Questions

My answer was right but it wasn't accepted.
Try the surname or full name. Capital letters, accents and apostrophes are ignored. If a common spelling still isn't accepted, email us and we'll add it.
How do I turn the music off?
Tap the speaker button at the top right. The app remembers your choice. Music is also silent when your iPhone's ring/silent switch is set to silent.
How do I reset my score?
Open the sport, then tap the ↺ button next to the score. Each sport keeps its own score.
Do I need an internet connection or an account?
No. The game works fully offline and has no accounts or sign-in.
I spotted a wrong clue.
Thank you! Please email us with the athlete's name and the clue, and we'll fix it in the next update.
