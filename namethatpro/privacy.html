Privacy Policy

Name That Pro · Effective October 30, 2026

Summary

Name That Pro does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves two things on your iPhone only:

  • your score (wins and games played) for each sport, and
  • whether you turned the background music on or off.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, software development kits, advertising or analytics tools.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
